The company spent approximately **₹259.28 million** on marketing campaigns during the analysis period.

### 2. Cost Per Click (CPC)

The average CPC is **₹22.91**, indicating the average amount spent for each customer click.

### 3. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

The average cost to acquire one customer is **₹12,523.92**.

### 4. Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)

The ROAS value is **5.01**, which means that every ₹1 spent on marketing generated approximately ₹5.01 in return.

- The campaigns generated a positive return on investment.
- Marketing spending resulted in a healthy ROAS.

- The calculated KPIs can help businesses evaluate campaign performance and optimize future marketing strategies.
